# ALASSAUL-AI V28 — Trabzon & Yomra Resident Focus

## Direction
Prioritize Trabzon and Yomra as the first local-service area for residents, while retaining the existing nationwide official portals and resident-service links.

## Added local map-search cards
- Sworn translators (Yeminli Tercüman)
- Health insurance offices for foreigners
- Auto repair workshops
- Auto spare-parts shops
- Accountants / certified public accountants (Mali Müşavir)
- Real-estate and apartment-management offices

## UI
- Added a distinct “دليل يومرا وطرابزون أولًا” local-focus panel.
- Responsive three-column grid that adapts to tablet and mobile widths.
- All local-service entries open map search results rather than claiming a specific branch, address, price, or opening status.
- User should confirm the provider's address, working hours, language support, and qualifications before visiting or paying.
