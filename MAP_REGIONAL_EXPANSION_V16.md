# ALASSAUL-AI V16 — Marmara regional map expansion

## Added city tabs and mapped destinations
- Yalova: city waterfront, Yalova thermal springs, Yuruyen Kosk, Sudusen Waterfall.
- Sapanca: Sapanca town/lake and Masukiye.
- Kartepe: mountain/ski area and Ormanya family nature park.
- Bursa: city center, Ulu Cami, Koza Han, Uludag.
- Sakarya: Adapazari city, Poyrazlar Lake, Justinian Bridge.
- Izmit: city waterfront, clock tower, Sekapark.
- Ulasli (Golcuk, Kocaeli): local coastal area, waterfront and nearby Degirmendere.

## Interaction
- The new locations appear in the existing city picker and use the same destination list, category filters, map markers, details panel and Google Maps directions action.
- Each city is shown separately so the map can focus on a smaller practical area rather than all of Marmara at once.
- Coordinates are approximate display anchors for map centering; visitors should use the directions link for navigation and verify seasonal access/hours for nature and mountain locations.

## Verification
- Source-level checks and archive integrity only. Full dependency-backed build, browser test, and live Google Maps API validation have not been run in this environment.
