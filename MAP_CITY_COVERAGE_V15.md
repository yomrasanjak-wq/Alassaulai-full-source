# ALASSAUL-AI V15 — City-aware interactive map

## Istanbul is a first-class destination
- Istanbul remains an explicit city tab alongside Trabzon, Rize, Ordu, Samsun, Giresun, Ankara, and Georgia.
- Added practical Istanbul destinations: Topkapi Palace, Basilica Cistern, Grand Bazaar, Taksim Square, Ortakoy Mosque, Camlica Hill, Pierre Loti Hill, and Miniaturk.
- Attractions are grouped by historic/Bosphorus area, shopping, views, nature, and family activities.
- Selecting Istanbul starts around the central/historic area instead of fitting all distant destinations (including islands and Florya) into one tiny map view.
- Selecting an attraction zooms closer to it; the directions button remains an intentional action to open Google Maps.

## Coverage
The map still includes the North Black Sea destinations and city/service areas: Samsun, Ordu, Giresun, Trabzon, Yomra, Arsin/Sürmene/Araklı region, Rize, and Ayder, plus Istanbul as a separate major destination.
